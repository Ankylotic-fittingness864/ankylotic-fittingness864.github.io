📌 What Is This?

stellaris-galaxy-forge is a desktop editor for your Stellaris galaxy maps. It's a tool that lets you see and change the galaxy of your saved game or a new game you're about to start. Think of it as a map editor – but for the stars.

You can move systems around, draw and cut hyperlanes (the lines connecting star systems), add new systems and nebulae, or even build a completely custom galaxy from scratch. It's designed for players who want to shape their universe beyond what the base game allows.

The best part? Only what you change is rewritten to your save file. Nothing else is touched. Plus, it creates backups and supports undo, so you can experiment freely without fear of breaking your game.

🔧 How It Works (Simple Version)

You don't need to understand the technical details to use this tool, but here's a quick look at what happens behind the scenes:

  • The app reads your Stellaris save file (or generates a new galaxy map).
  • It shows you a visual map of the galaxy with all systems and hyperlanes.
  • You make changes using simple click-and-drag actions.
  • When you save, the app carefully writes only your modifications back to the file, leaving everything else untouched.
  • A backup of the original file is always created first, so you're never stuck.

The tool is built with modern technology (Rust, React, and PixiJS) to ensure it's fast, stable, and responsive – even with large galaxies.
